## 3. FOUNDER STORY (long-form — for an "About" or "Story" page)

Diamonds run in the family. Prapanjj S K Kota comes from the Kota family, retailers under the "Kota" name for nearly a century, with his family's diamond trading roots running deep through Kota Diamonds — a partnership he joined in 2021 focused on natural and lab-grown loose diamond trading and bespoke jewellery.

But Prapanjj didn't just inherit the trade — he trained in it from the ground up. Before building a brand, he graded diamonds. As a Diamond Grader at S. Vinodkumar Diamonds Pvt Ltd and a Business Development Associate at De Beers Group, he learned the industry from the inside: how a stone is judged, how a supply chain moves, how a legacy business operates. He holds a Polished Diamond Graduate credential from JK Diamond Institute and a B.Com in International Finance from Christ University, Bangalore — a technical grounding in the stone itself, paired with a finance lens on the business.

In November 2022, that combination became Réia Diamonds — co-founded with Jacob Mathew and Ashwanth Ramesh. The bet: India's diamond retail experience hadn't caught up to how a new generation of buyers — especially urban, corporate women — actually wants to shop for the most meaningful purchase of their lives. Réia set out to replace the traditional "stock up and sell" jewellery counter with in-house designers at every outlet, CAD visualisation, 3D photorealistic rendering, and resin prototype (RPT) previews before a single stone is set — so customers see and approve their design before it's made.

Since its first flagship opened in Jayanagar, Bengaluru in July 2023, Réia has expanded to profitable stores in Bengaluru, Guwahati, and Coimbatore, opened India's first dedicated lab-grown diamond engagement ring studio on Dickenson Road, Bengaluru, and announced a 12-city flagship expansion in July 2025 to mark its second anniversary. In October 2025, the brand raised ₹2 crore in a pre-seed round led by Dinesh Talera and family (founders of Mysore Saree Udyog) with participation from Venture Catalysts — capital earmarked for retail expansion, deepening its omnichannel presence, and further innovation in engagement rings and everyday jewellery.

Under Prapanjj, the team has grown from three founders to 47+ people. He is also a Fellow of the Startup Leadership Program (since August 2023), and Réia's growth has been covered in outlets including The Retail Jeweller India, Entrepreneur India, The Tribune, India TV, and Medianews4u.

## 6. PHILOSOPHY / THOUGHT LEADERSHIP THEMES (for quote blocks, blog seeds, or a "Point of View" section)

**On what Réia stands for:**
Réia was built to offer what legacy brands typically don't — original designs, full customisation, transparent pricing, and a retail experience people genuinely enjoy, not just tolerate.

**On personalisation as the product:**
The customisation process is treated as a gold standard, not an upsell — every engraving, every taper of a ring band, every small personal detail a client wants preserved is deliberately captured, because of how significant the purchase is.

**On lab-grown diamonds:**
Lab-grown diamonds are chemically and optically identical to mined stones — the growth of the category is being driven by rising consumer education and a growing appetite for accessible luxury.

**On the target customer:**
Réia's core audience is the corporate, financially independent woman — the engagement ring is treated as a milestone purchase in her own life story, not only a gift to be given.

**On accessible luxury:**
Réia positions itself in the "sweet spot" between budget-consciousness and aspiration — serving buyers who won't compromise on quality but are increasingly value-conscious.

**On brand-building over selling:**
"From day one, we knew Réia couldn't be just another jewellery brand" — the company was built around original design, full customisation, transparent pricing, and an experience customers actually want, not a standard décor of a traditional jewellery counter.
